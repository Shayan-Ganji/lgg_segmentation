    \item \textbf{Data and Code Availability:} The imaging data utilized in this study are publicly accessible through The Cancer Imaging Archive (TCIA; Clark et al., 2013; Pedano et al., 2016) via the persistent DOI: \url{https://doi.org/10.7937/K9/TCIA.2016.L4LTD3TK} and mirrored on Kaggle at \texttt{https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mateuszbuda/lgg-mri-segmentation}. Matched clinical and genomic records were extracted from the companion table \texttt{data.csv} distributed with the benchmark dataset (Buda et al., 2019; Pedano et al., 2016), originally curated from the TCGA pan-glioma genomic study (Ceccarelli et al., 2016). All source code, reproduction scripts, and 5-fold cross-validation pipelines are openly maintained at GitHub: \url{https://github.com/Shayan-Ganji/lgg_segmentation}.
