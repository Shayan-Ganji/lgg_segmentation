Aims and Scope:
The Journal of Imaging Informatics in Medicine (JIIM) publishes peer-reviewed research that advances the science and application of imaging informatics in healthcare. Topics include:
- Machine learning, deep learning, and foundation models in diagnostic neuroimaging and oncology
- Rigorous data auditing, leakage detection, calibration, and reproducibility in medical image analysis
- Post-processing, volumetric 3D reconstruction, and clinical agreement metrics
- Radiogenomics, multi-institutional cohort validation, and clinical translation
- Standards, open-source computational tools, and ethical artificial intelligence

Submission Formatting Requirements:
- Title Page: Separate file containing Title, Authors, Affiliations, Corresponding Author contact, Conflict of Interest statement, Acknowledgments, Statements and Declarations (Funding, Competing Interests, Author Contributions, Ethics Approval, Consent to Participate/Publish, Data and Code Availability).
- Blinded Manuscript: Complete text, equations, tables, figures, and references with all author names, institutional affiliations, and identifying links strictly blinded.
- Full Manuscript: Complete text including author details for post-review production.
- Cover Letter: Addressed to Editor-in-Chief (Bradley J. Erickson, MD, PhD) highlighting core methodological and clinical contributions.
- References: Standard numbered or author-year Springer Nature reference style.
